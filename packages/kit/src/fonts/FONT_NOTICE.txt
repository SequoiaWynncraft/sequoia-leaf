Jersey 20 and Jersey 15 (jersey-20.woff2, jersey-15.woff2), the face of titles, names and buttons.

Copyright 2023 The Soft Type Project Authors (https://github.com/scfried/soft-type-jersey).
Version 1.001. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1; the licence travels with
them as OFL-Jersey.txt.

Provenance: the Latin files Google Fonts serves for the two families (fonts.gstatic.com,
jersey20/v4 and jersey15/v4), unchanged.

Each is drawn on a grid of its own and is sharp only at whole multiples of that size. The number
in a name is how many pixels tall its capitals are, not its size: Jersey 15 is 27 pixels to the em
(sharp at 27px, 54px, 81px) and Jersey 20 is 34 (sharp at 34px, 68px). Between those sizes its
pixels blur.

Inter (inter-variable.woff2), the face of everything read and of every number: one variable file,
weights 100 to 900.

Copyright (c) 2016 The Inter Project Authors (https://github.com/rsms/inter). Licensed under the
SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1; the licence travels with it as OFL-Inter.txt.

Provenance: a byte-identical copy of the file the Sequoia website ships in sequoia-web
frontend/static/assets/fonts/, where it sets the site's body text.

Minecraft Regular and Bold (minecraft-regular.woff2, minecraft-bold.woff2), only for words drawn
on a canvas in the world: names over heads, speech, signs. Nothing on the page is set in it.

Copyright (c) 2017 by Jacob Debono. Designer: Jacob Debono. Vendor: JDGraphics Fonts.
Version 1.000.

Licence statement embedded in the font files (OpenType name table, licence description, ID 13):
"Free for Commercial Use"

Provenance: byte-identical copies of the files the Sequoia website ships in sequoia-web
frontend/static/assets/fonts/. No separate licence text accompanies the fonts in that repository;
the embedded statement above is the licence information available.

It is drawn on a grid of ten pixels to the em, so it is sharp at 10px, 20px, 30px and so on.
