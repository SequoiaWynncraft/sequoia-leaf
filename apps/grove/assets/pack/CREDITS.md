# Block textures: Faithful 32x

The PNGs in this folder are from **Faithful 32x** (Java Edition, branch `java-latest`) by the
Faithful Resource Pack team: <https://faithfulpack.net>

- Source: <https://github.com/Faithful-Resource-Pack/Faithful-32x-Java>
- Licence: the Faithful License, Version 4, in `LICENSE.txt` beside this file (unmodified) and at
  <https://faithfulpack.net/license>

The files here are unchanged copies, and only the ones the game uses: the ground (grass block,
dirt, coarse dirt, podzol, moss), spruce, oak and dark oak logs and planks, spruce leaves and
sapling, cobblestone, mossy cobblestone, stone bricks, bricks, the ladder, glass, the barrel, fern,
short grass, the two mushrooms, four flowers and two wools.

`tools/pack.ts` turns them into `src/art/pack.gen.ts`. On the way it tints the pack's grey grass,
fern and spruce leaves, as a block game tints them by biome, makes a lighter and a darker spruce,
and makes the lit window from the pack's glass over a warm glow.
