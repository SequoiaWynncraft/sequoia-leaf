# Block textures: REFI Textures

The PNGs in this folder are from **REFI Textures** by MysticTempest, a 16px pack drawn from scratch
to recapture the look of Minecraft.

- Source: <https://github.com/MysticTempest/REFI_Textures>
- Listing: <https://content.luanti.org/packages/MysticTempest/refi_textures/>

They are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>). The files here are unchanged copies.

`tools/pack.ts` turns them into `src/art/pack.gen.ts`. It makes a few tiles from the pack's own on
the way: the grass block's side is the pack's dirt under its grass edge, the spruce leaves also come
in a lighter and a darker tone, and the window is the pack's glass over a warm glow. Those derived
tiles are under the same licence.

Anyone who ships the game must credit the pack as above. The licence covers these textures and
what is made from them, not the game's code.
