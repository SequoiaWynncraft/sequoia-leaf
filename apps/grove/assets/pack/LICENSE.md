# Block textures: Pixel Perfection

The PNGs in this folder are from **Pixel Perfection** by Hugh "XSSheep" Rutland, in its continued
edition **Pixel Perfection Legacy** by Nova_Wostra, with textures by freejusticehere and HexaBlu
(see the pack's own `pack.txt`).

- Original pack: <https://www.planetminecraft.com/texture-pack/131pixel-perfection/>
- This edition: <https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/pixel-perfection-legacy>, source
  <https://github.com/minetest-texture-packs/Pixel-Perfection-Legacy>

They are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>). The files here are unchanged copies.

`tools/pack.ts` turns them into `src/art/pack.gen.ts`. It changes some on the way: grey grass, ferns
and overlays are tinted with one grass colour, as a block game tints them by biome; spruce leaves
are also made in a lighter and a darker tone; the window is the pack's glass over a warm glow. Those
derived tiles are under the same licence.

Anyone who ships the game must credit the pack as above. The licence covers these textures and
what is made from them, not the game's code.
